⚠ Single-GPU rollout note
  KServe v0.14 creates the predictor Deployment with RollingUpdate. With one
  GPU, a new pod can't surge — old must die first. After the first install,
  patch the strategy once:

    kubectl patch deployment {{ .Values.model.servedName }}-predictor \
      -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} --type=strategic \
      -p '{"spec":{"strategy":{"type":"Recreate","rollingUpdate":null}}}'

Smoke-test the OpenAI-compatible API:
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    svc/{{ .Values.model.servedName }}-predictor 18080:80 &
  curl http://localhost:18080/v1/models
